Routing Replay

EXPTIME level generator, crossover template
What am I looking at?

The map is one crossover block of the NetHack level (grey = corridors, dots = boulders). Coloured boxes are gadget tiles (blue = diode, gold = SCD). The generator draws a "check path": a curve that runs through the wall between corridors and must visit every tile, cutting each gadget leg twice (first at its Wt slot, then on the way back at its Wb slot) and then attaching the check boxes (SO/SD) by short wires.

Each animation is one search ("attempt"): the curve grows from its current head towards the next target. green = a route was found and stays; red = no route: the red haze is everything the search could reach, the red line goes to the closest point it got to the target (rings). After failures the generator backs up (undoes pieces) and tries another variant or tile order. Muted lines: level-2 chain, level-1 chain, wires.
